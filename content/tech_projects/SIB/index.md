## Overview
This project involved designing and analyzing sociolinguistic datasets to investigate variation in Spanish speech. It demonstrates end-to-end experience in data creation, annotation design, quality assurance, and statistical analysis.

## My Role
- Designed [annotation guidelines](https://leeannvidal.github.io/documents/Portfolio/Vidal_Covas_Liquids_Coding_Manual.pdf) for novel linguistic variables 
- Managed dataset collection, curation, and QA workflows  
- Supervised and trained student annotators  
- Led full research lifecycle from data design to statistical modeling  

## Data & Methods
- Built and analyzed datasets of 70k+ tokens  
- Conducted coding, [extraction](https://github.com/leeannvidal/leeannvidal.github.io/blob/main/code/Extract_Annotations_to_CSV.praat), and [statistical analysis](https://github.com/leeannvidal/dissertation_data_analysis/tree/main) in R  
- Applied [probabilistic modeling](https://github.com/leeannvidal/dissertation_data_analysis/tree/main/scripts/statistical_analysis/regressions/liquids) to investigate linguistic variation  
- Developed workflows for annotation consistency and data quality  

## Outcome
- Produced structured datasets for analyzing Spanish variation  
- Generated findings contributing to [dissertation research](https://hdl.handle.net/2144/51243)  
- Demonstrated scalable approaches to linguistic data annotation and QA
